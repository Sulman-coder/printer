About Us

clear-prints.com is your trusted online store for refurbished and affordable printers. We specialize in providing customers with high-quality, professionally tested printers that deliver excellent performance at a fraction of the cost of new models.

Founded with a simple mission — to make reliable printing accessible and sustainable — we help individuals and businesses save money while reducing electronic waste. Each printer we sell goes through a professional inspection, cleaning, and testing process before it reaches you.

What We Do

  • Sell refurbished, used, and second-hand printers that are inspected for quality and performance.
  • Provide clear setup guides and helpful information for connecting your printer at home or office.
  • Offer warranty-backed printers with transparent return and refund policies.
  • Promote eco-friendly printing by giving used devices a new life instead of ending up as waste.

Our Promise

We’re committed to honesty, transparency, and quality service. When you shop with us, you can expect:

  • Upfront pricing with no hidden fees
  • Detailed product descriptions and clear condition grades
  • Responsive customer support
  • Environmentally responsible operations
